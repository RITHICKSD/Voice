Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can I get custom auditions for my script?

For standard commercial and corporate scripts submitted during studio hours, we deliver 3 custom audio auditions within 2 to 4 hours free of charge.

Can we direct the voice artist live via remote audio link?

Absolutely. We support real-time high-definition direction via Source-Connect Pro X, Zoom, IPDTL, or phone patch with a dedicated sound engineer monitoring the session.

What file formats do you deliver?

We deliver uncompressed 24-Bit / 96kHz or 48kHz WAV files, split stem tracks, video sync audio files, and ACX-compliant MP3 files depending on your specification.
